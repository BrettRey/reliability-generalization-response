What changes when the counts change?

The formulas and what they retain

Correlation uses the sum of cross-products and both sums of squares. Adding a constant or multiplying by a positive constant throughout either series preserves r, when it is defined. Rescaling all counts is different from changing the sampled population's range.

Exact agreement = 100 × (number of pairs with Aᵢ = Bᵢ) ÷ 4.

Each pair becomes 1 for a match or 0 for a mismatch. Totals of one, two, or three don't identify which clips matched. Zero means all pairs differ; four means all pairs match and B is identical to A. Mismatch sizes are discarded. Exact matching is the chosen rule for these integer counts.
